How It Works

From Document to Print-Ready in Minutes.

No templates. No manual setup. No reprints.

Step 1 — Select

Drop in your documents.

Drag and drop your PDF or DOCX files into the workspace. You can select multiple files at once — pleadings, exhibits, or combined sets. ExhibitPro handles them all in one go.

Step 2 — Extract

ExhibitPro does the reading.

No tagging. No highlighting. No manual work. ExhibitPro scans your documents, detects every exhibit identifier and heading, and builds a perfectly aligned set automatically. What used to take hours takes minutes.

Step 3 — Review

Adjust anything. Change nothing if it’s already right.

Review your labels and tabs before printing. Remove extras, adjust formatting, fine-tune spacing on the right panel. Most of the time you won’t need to touch a thing.

Step 4 — Print

Preview, confirm, done.

See exactly how your output will look before it hits the printer. Confirm alignment, select your preferred exhibit base sheet, and print. Print-ready in one click.
